Introduction

In the current digital landscape, where information is widely accessible and content creation tools are more powerful than ever, maintaining originality and authenticity has become a significant challenge. Plagiarism, in its many forms, continues to threaten the integrity of academic work, professional research, journalism, and creative writing. With the increasing availability of online resources and the rapid rise of artificial intelligence (AI) tools capable of generating human-like content, distinguishing between original and duplicated material has grown considerably more complex.

The Text Purity – Plagiarism Detection System is a modern, multi-functional web application developed to address these challenges by offering a comprehensive platform for detecting various forms of content duplication. Unlike traditional plagiarism detection systems that focus solely on matching text against known databases, this system integrates several advanced technologies to enhance its accuracy and effectiveness. It combines textual similarity analysis, image content comparison, and AI-generated text detection into a unified, user-friendly solution.

One of the key distinguishing features of this system is its ability to analyze content across different media types. Users can upload not only text documents but also image files, enabling the system to perform checks for both textual and visual plagiarism. For textual analysis, the system employs Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques and vector-based similarity measures to detect paraphrased or semantically similar content that may bypass conventional keyword-matching algorithms. For image comparison, it extracts visual features and uses embedding-based techniques to identify possible instances of reused, altered, or replicated graphical content.

Moreover, the emergence of AI-generated text introduces a new dimension of complexity in content authenticity. Tools such as ChatGPT, GPT-4, and other large language models are capable of producing highly coherent and convincing text, often indistinguishable from human writing. As a countermeasure, the Text Purity system integrates an AI-detection module that evaluates the likelihood that a piece of content has been generated by AI. This functionality is especially relevant in academic settings, where AI-generated submissions may compromise academic integrity if not properly cited.

The system supports a wide range of commonly used file formats, including .txt, .docx, and .pdf for textual content, and .jpeg, .jpg, and .png for images. This makes it highly accessible and versatile for a variety of users. The web-based interface simplifies the process of uploading and analyzing files, presenting results in an informative format that includes similarity scores, visual indicators, and feedback on potential plagiarism or AI involvement.

This project contributes meaningfully to the ongoing global efforts aimed at promoting originality, transparency, and academic honesty. By offering a more intelligent and adaptable approach to plagiarism detection, the system serves the needs of educators, institutions, researchers, and content reviewers alike—providing them with a powerful tool to verify the authenticity of diverse forms of content.

By addressing not only conventional plagiarism but also visual and AI-generated duplication, the Text Purity – Plagiarism Detection System stands as a relevant, robust, and forward-thinking solution for modern content verification challenges.

1.1 Background

The proliferation of digital technologies has revolutionized the way information is created, shared, and consumed. In academia, research, journalism, and professional writing, digital content has become the primary medium of communication. While this digital shift offers numerous benefits, such as ease of access, collaboration, and automation, it also introduces serious challenges related to content authenticity and intellectual property.

Plagiarism—defined as the unauthorized use or close imitation of another author’s work and presenting it as one’s own—has become increasingly common due to the ease with which information can be copied and reused from online sources. Many students, content creators, and even professionals unintentionally or deliberately replicate content without proper citation, leading to ethical concerns and reputational risks.

Compounding this issue is the rapid rise of generative AI technologies such as ChatGPT and other large language models, which can produce high-quality text that is difficult to distinguish from human writing. These tools, while useful, make it even more challenging to ensure that submitted content is original. Additionally, the problem is not limited to text alone; visual content such as diagrams, charts, and images are frequently reused without acknowledgment, often escaping the scrutiny of standard plagiarism detectors.

Traditional plagiarism detection tools primarily rely on keyword or phrase matching techniques, which are limited in their ability to detect paraphrased content, semantic similarities, AI-generated content, or image duplication. There is a growing need for intelligent systems that can address these limitations and offer a comprehensive solution that adapts to modern content creation trends.

The Text Purity – Plagiarism Detection System was conceptualized in response to this need. It integrates multiple advanced techniques such as natural language processing, image similarity analysis, and AI-detection algorithms into a unified platform. The goal is to provide a robust, scalable, and accessible tool for detecting various forms of plagiarism and promoting content originality in the digital age.
1.2 Objectives

The primary goal of this project is to design and implement an intelligent, multi-functional plagiarism detection system that is capable of handling a wide range of content formats and identifying different forms of content duplication. The specific objectives are as follows:

To develop a web-based application that enables users to upload and analyze both textual and visual content for potential plagiarism.

To implement text plagiarism detection using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and semantic similarity techniques that can identify not just direct copying, but also paraphrased or semantically similar content.

To build an image comparison module that detects reused or slightly altered images using embedding-based analysis and visual feature extraction.

To integrate an AI-content detection mechanism capable of evaluating whether the submitted text was generated by AI language models, thus addressing the modern challenge of AI-assisted plagiarism.

To support multiple commonly used file formats, including .txt, .docx, .pdf, .jpeg, .jpg, and .png, for both textual and image-based analysis.

To provide a clear and user-friendly web interface where users can view similarity scores, detailed results, and interpretation of plagiarism or AI likelihood in a meaningful way.

To ensure the system is modular, scalable, and deployable, allowing for future integration with larger databases or institutional learning platforms.
